Crockpot Mongolian Beef & Noodles

Source: Cool Kitchen, Bro! — Serves 6, ~8 hr (slow cooker)

Ingredients

  • 2 lbs chuck roast
  • 1 tbsp avocado or vegetable oil
  • 1/2 tsp black pepper
  • 1/4 tsp salt
  • 1/2 cup low-sodium soy sauce
  • 1/2 cup beef broth
  • 1/3 cup brown sugar
  • 1/4 cup hoisin sauce
  • 1 tbsp minced garlic
  • 1 tsp ground ginger
  • 1 tbsp sesame oil
  • 1 tbsp sriracha
  • 2 cups shredded carrots
  • 2 tbsp cornstarch
  • 2 tbsp cold water
  • 2-3 packages ramen noodles (seasoning packets discarded)
  • green onions, sliced, for garnish
  • 1 tbsp sesame seeds
  • chili crisp or chili oil, to taste

Instructions

  1. Season the two pounds of chuck roast, cut into 1½–2-inch chunks, with ½ teaspoon black pepper and ¼ teaspoon salt. Heat 1 tablespoon avocado or vegetable oil in a skillet and sear the beef for 1–2 minutes per side to develop a deep, caramelized crust; you can skip the sear if pressed for time, but that extra depth of flavor is worth the effort.
  2. Transfer the seared beef to the crockpot. Add ½ cup low-sodium soy sauce, ½ cup beef broth, ⅓ cup packed brown sugar, ¼ cup hoisin sauce, 1 tablespoon minced garlic, 1 teaspoon ground ginger, 1 tablespoon sesame oil, 1 tablespoon sriracha, and 2 cups shredded carrots.
  3. Cover and cook on LOW for 6–8 hours or HIGH for 3–4 hours, until the beef is fall-apart tender. Break the beef into bite-size pieces with two forks.
  4. Whisk 2 tablespoons cornstarch with 2 tablespoons cold water and stir into the crockpot. Cover and cook on HIGH for another 10–15 minutes to thicken the sauce.
  5. Add 2–3 packages ramen noodles (seasoning packets discarded). Push the noodles into the sauce, cover, and cook another 10–15 minutes, stirring halfway through. Add an extra ¼–½ cup beef broth if needed.
  6. Toss everything together and finish with sliced green onions, sesame seeds, and a drizzle of chili crisp or chili oil before serving.
