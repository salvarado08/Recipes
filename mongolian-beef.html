Mongolian Beef

Source: Mongolian Beef Recipe Book — Serves 4, ~30 min

Ingredients

  • 1 cup uncooked rice
  • 1.5 lb steak
  • 3/4 cup soy sauce
  • 3 tbsp vegetable oil
  • 1 tbsp fresh ginger
  • 3 cloves garlic
  • 1/2 cup green onions
  • 1 tbsp brown sugar
  • 1 tbsp sesame seeds

Instructions

  1. Cook rice according to package directions. Set aside.
  2. Slice steak into thin strips against the grain.
  3. In a bowl, mix soy sauce and brown sugar until dissolved.
  4. In a large pan, heat vegetable oil over medium-high heat.
  5. Add steak to the pan and stir-fry until browned, about 3-4 minutes.
  6. Add ginger and garlic, cooking for another minute.
  7. Pour in the soy sauce mixture and cook for 2-3 minutes until sauce thickens.
  8. Stir in green onions and sprinkle with sesame seeds before serving.
  9. Serve stir-fry over rice and enjoy!
